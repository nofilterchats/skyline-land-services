Common Questions

What Is an Easement?

An easement is a legal right for another party, such as a utility or public agency, to use a portion of your property for a specific purpose, while you retain ownership of the land itself.

Eminent Domain, Explained

Eminent domain is the government's power to acquire private property for public use, provided the owner receives just compensation. There is a formal process, and owners have rights throughout it.

How Compensation Is Calculated

Compensation is generally based on fair market value, considering the portion of land taken, any damage to the remaining property, and the impact on its use.
